7-chapter learning path

Level 1 — Chapter Index

By Sahil Pekde · Seven practical lessons in accounting foundations

Start with the accounting records that underpin every financial decision. Work through the seven chapters in order, or open a specific topic.

Chapter 01 · Accounting Principles and Double-Entry BookkeepingUnderstand debit, credit, accounting principles and double-entry bookkeeping through practical transactions.Chapter 02 · Chart of Accounts and Account ClassificationDesign and classify assets, liabilities, equity, income and expenses for reliable reporting.Chapter 03 · Journal Entries, Ledgers and ReconciliationsRecord journal entries, post to ledgers and reconcile banks, customers and suppliers.Chapter 04 · Trial Balance and Month-End ClosingPrepare a trial balance, post adjustments and complete a controlled month-end close.Chapter 05 · Preparing the P&L, Balance Sheet and Cash Flow StatementPrepare and connect the profit and loss statement, balance sheet and cash flow statement.Chapter 06 · Financial Statement Accuracy and Quality ChecksDetect misclassifications, missing provisions, reconciliation differences and reporting errors.Chapter 07 · Internal Controls and Audit ReadinessBuild approval controls, segregation of duties, audit trails and audit-ready documentation.
Suggested learning method: Read the explanation, reproduce the entries or calculations yourself, complete the knowledge check and consider the CFO perspective. Each lesson works on mobile and desktop.
