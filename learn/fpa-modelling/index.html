Level 3 — Chapter Index

By Sahil Pekde · Seven practical lessons in FP&A and financial modelling

Go beyond calculating ratios. Learn how to validate financial statements, diagnose performance, assess risk and turn insights into actions. Follow the chapters in order or choose a topic.

Chapter 01 · Budgeting FundamentalsBuild an operating budget from commercial drivers, costs and cash requirements.Chapter 02 · Revenue and Cost ForecastingTranslate pipeline, occupancy, pricing, headcount and contracts into forecasts.Chapter 03 · Budget vs Actual Variance AnalysisExplain performance gaps using price, volume, mix, timing and cost drivers.Chapter 04 · Rolling Forecasts and Scenario PlanningUpdate the outlook continuously and test base, upside and downside cases.Chapter 05 · Cash-Flow ForecastingBuild a 13-week cash forecast and identify funding gaps before they occur.Chapter 06 · Integrated Three-Statement Financial ModellingConnect projected profit, balance sheet and cash flow through consistent schedules.Chapter 07 · Management Reporting and KPI DashboardsTurn forecasts and actuals into concise, decision-oriented management reports.
Suggested learning method: Review the formula, reproduce the worked example, explain the business implication, and complete the knowledge check.
