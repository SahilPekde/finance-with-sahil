Profitability Ratios

By Sahil Pekde · Interpret margins, returns and the drivers of operating profitability.

1. Margins reveal the economics of the business

Profitability analysis explains how much of each rupee of revenue remains after different layers of cost. Use consistent definitions of revenue, direct cost, EBITDA and operating profit.

2. Core profitability ratios

MetricFormulaWhat it reveals
Gross margin(Revenue − direct cost) ÷ RevenuePricing and direct delivery efficiency
EBITDA marginEBITDA ÷ RevenueOperating earnings before depreciation and amortisation
EBIT marginEBIT ÷ RevenueOperating earnings after depreciation/amortisation
Net marginProfit after tax ÷ RevenueBottom-line profitability
ROCEEBIT ÷ average capital employedOperating return on invested long-term capital

4. What could explain a falling margin?

Investigate price changes, customer mix, occupancy/utilisation, supplier inflation, employee productivity, discounts and exceptional items. EBITDA excludes some material costs and is not the same as operating cash flow.

CFO perspective: Present a margin bridge: last period margin → pricing impact → volume/mix → direct cost inflation → overhead → current margin.
Try it yourself: Revenue ₹150 lakh and EBITDA ₹24 lakh: EBITDA margin = 16%. If EBITDA remains unchanged while revenue grows to ₹180 lakh, margin falls to 13.33%.
