Level 2 — Chapter Index

By Sahil Pekde · Eight practical lessons in financial analysis

Go beyond calculating ratios. Learn how to validate financial statements, diagnose performance, assess risk and turn insights into actions. Follow the chapters in order or choose a topic.

Chapter 01 · How to Read and Validate Financial StatementsUnderstand what financial statements reveal and verify the reliability of their underlying numbers.Chapter 02 · Horizontal and Vertical AnalysisCompare financial performance across periods and analyse each line as a percentage of a meaningful base.Chapter 03 · Liquidity RatiosMeasure short-term financial resilience using current, quick and cash ratios.Chapter 04 · Profitability RatiosInterpret margins, returns and the drivers of operating profitability.Chapter 05 · Solvency and Leverage RatiosAssess borrowing risk, debt-servicing capacity and balance-sheet resilience.Chapter 06 · Receivables and Payables AgeingUse ageing schedules to evaluate collections, overdue balances and supplier obligations.Chapter 07 · Working Capital and Cash Conversion CycleCalculate operating working capital and the cash conversion cycle.Chapter 08 · Trend Analysis and Management InsightsTurn financial trends into management insights, forecasts and corrective actions.
Suggested learning method: Review the formula, reproduce the worked example, explain the business implication, and complete the knowledge check.
