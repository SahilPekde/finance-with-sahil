About this project

Finance learning, made practical

I'm Sahil Pekde, a finance professional passionate about understanding how financial information supports better business decisions.

I created Finance with Sahil to share practical learning resources, financial models and clear explanations—from accounting fundamentals to financial leadership. This website is also a record of continuous learning and improvement.

Our approach: understand the concept, work through an example, and apply it to a business decision.
